## Part A: How an individual accepts

An individual accepts this agreement through the CLA check on their first pull
request: sign in with GitHub, fill in the requested details and accept. The
acceptance applies to that GitHub account and records this agreement's
version. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#the-contributor-license-agreement).

## Part B: Entity contributions

A company or other legal entity whose employees or contractors contribute
within the scope of their work accepts this agreement on its own behalf.

1. A person authorised to bind the entity signs this agreement, naming the
   entity, its address, a point of contact (the "CLA Manager") and the GitHub
   accounts of the people authorised to contribute on its behalf (its
   "Designated Contributors"), and sends it to the Maintainer.
2. Sections 1 to 13 then apply with the entity as "You", covering
   Contributions its Designated Contributors Submit on its behalf.
3. The CLA Manager tells the Maintainer when Designated Contributors are added
   or removed. Removing one does not affect Contributions already Submitted.
4. Each Designated Contributor also accepts Part A with their own GitHub
   account, stating that they contribute on behalf of the entity. Their
   acceptance records the GitHub identity; the entity's signature supplies the
   rights.
